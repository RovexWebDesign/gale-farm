Grow With Us

Grow the Future With Us.

Growing Is for Learning

Gale Farm's growing program is designed for children ages 5–18. Whether a child has gardened before or has never planted a seed, this is an opportunity to learn and experience the growing process.

Gale Farm members teach participants and provide the tools students need for the growing activities. Children get the opportunity to learn by doing, build patience, and understand the care that goes into growing food.

What Children Can Experience

Learn by Doing

Children can learn about growing through hands-on activities and guidance from Gale Farm members.

Tools Provided

Tools are provided for students participating in the growing activities.

Beginners Welcome

No previous gardening experience is needed. Children can come ready to learn.

Care Comes First

Children may take home what they grow when they have taken care of it.

Who Can Participate?

The program is for children ages 5 through 18. Children who are not already students in the program must submit a request to participate.

Interested in joining?

Contact Gale Farm to ask about the program and how to submit a request.
