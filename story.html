Our Story

Every meaningful journey starts somewhere. Ours started with a piece of land and a purpose.

A Vision Planted With Faith

Gale Farm began when Myrna Gale bought a piece of land because she felt God wanted her to. What may have looked like an ordinary piece of land held the possibility of something much bigger.

Myrna Gale is completely involved in the farm, and the entire family helps run it. Together, they are building something that is about more than what can be planted in the soil. It is about family, learning, community, and creating something meaningful that can be shared.

Gale Farm is at the beginning of its growing journey, with planting planned for April 2027. The vision reaches far beyond one season: to create a place that can be loved, cared for, and enjoyed by generations to come.

“What we plant today can become a blessing for generations to come.”

— A Gale Farm saying

Built Around Family

From Myrna Gale's vision to the hands of the family helping bring it to life, Gale Farm is a shared effort. Each person has a part in helping the farm grow into the place it is meant to become.

Our hope is that the farm will be a place where people can connect with the land, children can learn through growing, and fresh food can bring people together.
