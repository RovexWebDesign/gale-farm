Life at Gale Farm

A place where family, learning, creativity, and growing come together.

A Farm in the Making

Gale Farm is more than a piece of land. It is a growing vision built with love, family, faith, and a desire to bring people together. What began as land purchased by Myrna Gale has become a place where the entire family works together to build something meaningful. Every step brings a new opportunity to learn, grow, and create lasting memories.

Our Farm Life

The Beginning

What began as a simple piece of land has grown into a vision filled with love, purpose, and endless possibilities. With Myrna Gale's dream leading the way and the entire family working together, Gale Farm is becoming a place where generations can come together, learn new things, and create lasting memories. Every effort, every idea, and every step forward brings this vision closer to life. Together, they are building more than a farm; they are building something meaningful that can be shared for years to come!

Preparing to Grow

Our next chapter is all about preparing for planting and bringing the vision of Gale Farm one step closer to life. There is still much work to be done as we prepare the land, plan for the growing season, and get everything in place for the seeds that will soon take root. Every step, no matter how small, is part of building something meaningful for the family and the community. As the farm continues to develop, we look forward to sharing more of the journey, including the progress, preparations, and exciting moments along the way. This is only the beginning, and there is so much more to come at Gale Farm!

Children's Learning Book

A special group book will be created to showcase what the children learn throughout their growing experiences at Gale Farm. As they explore gardening, discover how vegetables grow, and care for their plants, their experiences will become part of a shared learning journey. The book will celebrate their knowledge, creativity, progress, and the special memories they make along the way. It will also give them something meaningful to look back on and feel proud of as they continue to grow and learn at Gale Farm!

More to Come

Gale Farm is still growing, and there are many exciting things ahead! As we continue preparing the land, planning future activities, and building opportunities for families and children, new experiences will gradually become part of the farm. We look forward to sharing each milestone, celebrating our progress, and welcoming new ideas along the way. Every step brings us closer to the vision we have for Gale Farm, and this is only the beginning of something special!

Everyone Can Enjoy the Harvest

Not everyone has the time, space, or ability to grow their own vegetables, and that's okay! At Gale Farm, we believe everyone should have the opportunity to enjoy fresh, locally grown produce. You may not be able to plant a seed, but you can still support the farm and enjoy the harvest by purchasing our vegetables. There's a place for everyone at Gale Farm!

Growing Knowledge

Gale Farm is more than a place to grow vegetables; it's a place to learn. Through hands-on experiences, children ages 5–18 can discover how plants grow, understand where their food comes from, and learn the care and patience that farming requires. From planting seeds to caring for growing vegetables, every step offers a chance to build new skills, ask questions, and gain knowledge. At Gale Farm, learning doesn't just happen in a classroom. IT CAN HAPPEN RIGHT HERE IN THE GARDEN!

Creative Writing Publication

Myrna Gale is the Founder and CEO of 3G Publishing, Inc. Her passion for children inspired her to develop a company that would keep the basics of reading, writing, and creativity a top priority for children, adolescents, and adults. Ms. Gale previously worked in the legal environment for nearly three decades. Although her passion for the law remains, she is now combining her passion for creative writing and publishing into her repertoire, bringing 3G Publishing, Inc. to a major success. With the success of 3G Publishing, Inc. she is now creating the Gale Farm, will she will be teaching children ages 5-18 how to develop a love for farming, and also sharing with others through creative writing the love of farming.
